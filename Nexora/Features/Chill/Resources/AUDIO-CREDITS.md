# Chill audio resources

The five bundled recordings are stored as AAC/M4A files for native iOS playback. Each one has a two-second crossfade at the loop boundary and loudness normalization. The original recordings and license details are:

- Rain: “Garden rainfall” by Lionel Allorge, sourced from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Garden_rainfall.ogg), under the [Free Art License 1.3](https://artlibre.org/licence/lal/en/).
- Fireplace: “Fire burning” by Gpkp, sourced from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fire_burning.webm), licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The converted file is offered under the same license.
- Waves: “Waves” by Dsw4, sourced from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Waves.ogg), released into the public domain by its author.
- Forest: “Forest ambience” by Gravity Sound, sourced from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Forest_ambience_(Gravity_Sound).wav), licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- Night: “Oecanthus pellucens song 23C” by Gunther Tschuch, recorded at 9 p.m., sourced from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oecanthus_pellucens_song_23C.wav), licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The converted file is offered under the same license.

No recording is generated from silence or used solely as a keepalive. “Sans son” does not activate a background audio session.
